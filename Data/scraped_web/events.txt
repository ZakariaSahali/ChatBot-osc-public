# Moments That Matter

From hands-on workshops to exciting challenges, our events bring students together to learn, grow, and create unforgettable experiences.

## Our Events

SIGNATURES EVENTS

### Hack2Night

Hack2Night is an annual cybersecurity event that features conferences, talks, networking opportunities, and Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions.

### GAME DEV OSC

Game Dev Competition was an exciting challenge that encouraged participants to turn their ideas into playable games, fostering creativity, teamwork, and technical skills in game development.

SPECIAL EVENTS

### SUMMMER SKILLS

A summer camp for various computer science fields such as apps development, cybersecurity, graphic design and more.

### Chess Tournament

A Chess Tournament at the faculty of sciences, as a year-opening event.

### RAMADAN NIGHTS

An online event (usually on discord), held during the holy month of ramadan every year, where nights are animated with either a workshop, course, or even game night or anime/film party.

CELEBRATIONS EVENTS

### PI DAY

A celebration day of the famous Pi number that is yearly worldwide celebrated on 14/03, a day filled with captivating conferences and stimulants activities.

COLLABORATIONS EVENTS

### SAHRA SCIENCE

A monthly event hosted by the university's clubs, where students, teachers, guests and scientists gather a roundtable talk on a particular topic of sciences.
