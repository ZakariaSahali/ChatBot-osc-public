## Who can join

A university student in Algeria.

A student with a great motivation to give and receive from the club.

A student with a decent commitment to the club.

## How to join us

### Registration

* Fill out the registration form once it opens.
* Receive our response by email.
* Confirm your attendance and prepare.

### Interview

* Show up as agreed.
* Bring your best projects, if you have any.
* Places are limited, be motivated, be yourself.

### Response

* Get your final response by email within days.
* Rejected? You're still part of our community.
* Accepted? Welcome to the family!

## Frequently Asked Questions

When Will The Registrations Be Opened ?

Registrations are opened from once to twice a year, usually in the beginning of the academic year and/or in the second semester of the academic year.

Can I Join The Club Even If I'm Not A Computer Science Student ?

Yes, you can, students of any field or domain can join the club.

Can Students From Other Universities Join The Club ?

Of course, students from across Algeria can join us.

Where Is Your Local (HQ) ?

Our Head-Quarters (Local) is situated in the Faculty of Sciences of University of Algiers 1 in Algiers Center in Algeria, precisely in the garden under the Mathematics Department.

How Is The Club Structured ?

The club's structure is tree-like, President in head, Vice-President, Treasurer, followed by the different departments.
